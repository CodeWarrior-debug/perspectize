Perspectives pages

Throwaway preview · fake data · issues #751 and #752

What this proposes

Answers to the open questions in #751 and #752, drawn so they can be argued with. None of it is decided.

Two views, one nav entry

"By content" (#751) and "By perspective" (#752) are two routes under one nav item. They share the metric names, the ruler mark and the privacy rules, but keep their own filters, sorts and default columns, as #752 asks. A content row drills down into the second view, filtered to that item. Opening a row there reads the perspective.

Aggregators

  • Average, spread (standard deviation), lowest, highest and count are on offer. A chip in each header switches that column; "use for all columns" sets the rest.
  • Sum is listed but disabled for the four rating dimensions: adding up 0–10 ratings means nothing. It stays for count-like and custom numeric fields later.
  • On a phone the aggregator is page-wide, set from a sheet, because per-column chips do not fit a card.
  • Median and percentiles are left for later.

Minimum number of people

Average, spread, lowest and highest show only once three people have rated that metric. Below that the cell shows a dash and how many so far, because one person's value would otherwise be readable from the number. Count is always shown. "Just mine" has no minimum. The number three is a placeholder for the spec.

Whose perspectives

"Everyone" counts the public perspectives plus your own private ones: what you can read, not what exists. "Just mine" is your own. Friends and share lists wait for the sharing model (ADR 0002). Defaults: Everyone on "By content", Just mine on "By perspective" (as #752 says).

People, not perspectives

One person with a public and a private perspective on the same item counts once, public first. Cells say "n" for people who rated that metric, and the People column counts people, not rows. "Forty Years of One Tree" shows it: five perspectives, four people.

Missing is not zero

An item nobody has rated on a metric shows a dash and "n 0", never 0.000. Each cell carries its own n, because a perspective can leave a dimension blank ("What the Tides Do to a Harbour").

Spread and "split"

Each cell draws the ratings people gave as dots on a 0–10 line. Average and highest/lowest add a tick; spread shades one standard deviation either side of the mean. A wide spread adds the word "split" beside the number. Marks stay one colour: the rating colours (green and red) are only used for thumbs.

Private marks

Private perspectives carry a lock and the word "Private", as Compare does. On a perspective you hold both slots of, the page offers "Public take" and "Private notes" (names from the multiple-perspectives spec, still to be confirmed).

Name and nav

"Perspectives" is the working label (the other candidate in #751 was "Insights"). It sits beside Library here. Where it lives belongs to the flow-based navigation work (#722), and the phone header ignores the overflow problem in #840 on purpose.

Not drawn

  • The Sort, Columns and Export controls (dashed). They reuse the Library's pickers; Export is #800.
  • The perspective editor. Edit opens the existing one.
  • Loading, error and signed-out states.

Ratings are stored 0–10000 and shown 0–10 to three places, as in the app.
