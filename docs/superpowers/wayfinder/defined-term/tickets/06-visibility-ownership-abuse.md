# Who sees a custom definition

Type: `wayfinder:grilling` (HITL) · Status: open · Blocked by: [Definition identity, editing and lazy creation](05-definition-identity-and-editing.md) · Claimed by: none

## Question

Content is shared: a definition added by one person exists for everyone, while perspectives carry the privacy setting. Is a custom definition therefore public the moment it is added, or can someone keep a private definition, such as a personal take on "worship" they have not shared? Settle who owns a definition (the adder, as `addedByUserID`), who can edit or remove it, how a definition nobody agrees with is handled, and what protects against abusive or spam definitions on a shared term. Read [ADR 0002: sharing model](../../../adr/0002-sharing-model.md) first.
