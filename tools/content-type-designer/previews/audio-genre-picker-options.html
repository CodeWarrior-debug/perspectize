Genre picker: three ways to shape Apple's genre tree

All three pickers are built from the same Apple Music genre tree (snapshot 2026-10-10, 525 genres under the Music root). Open each, search, expand the foldouts, and compare what a pick looks like and what would be stored.

Try the same genre in all three

Click a genre to select it in both pickers. Deep ones (such as Dabke or French Baroque) show how Option B folds them into a parent. Cuban and Dabke show where Option C files ethnicity-based genres.

Source: Apple's store-services genre tree (an unofficial, undocumented endpoint, so a snapshot would be copied into the database once instead of fetched live). Apple's own names and ids are kept. Nothing here is decided or built. Option A keeps every Apple level. Option B folds to two levels. Option C keeps every level but files ethnicity, region and language genres under Worldwide to shorten the top-level list.
